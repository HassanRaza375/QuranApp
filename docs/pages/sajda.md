# Page: Verses of prostration (Sajda)

- Route (legacy, kept): `/sajda`
- Phase: 9B
- Prototype section: `d-page-sajda` / `m-page-sajda`
- Offline level: A for the list and references (bundled); B for the Arabic (the reader's cache, then the per-ayah cache).
- Indexable: yes.

## Purpose

See the verses of prostration in the Qur'an with their locations, read them, save them, and open them in the reader.

## Layout

- One `h1` "Verses of prostration" and "15 verses, as listed by this app’s data."
- An ordered list of the 15 verses in Qur'an order. Each row: the number, the surah name and "surah:ayah" (from the data), "Juz N · Page N" (from the juz and page starts), a Save star (44px), the Arabic (the surah reader's text, `lang="ar"`, right to left), and "Open in the reader" (`/surah/N#ayah-M`).

## What is shown and what is deliberately not

| Shown | Not shown (until you supply a reviewed source) |
| --- | --- |
| The service's 15 references, bundled | Any statement that a prostration is obligatory or recommended (the legacy page showed the service's flags as red and blue labels to everyone: four verses were "Obligatory", eleven "Recommended") |
| Juz and page, derived from the app's tables (checked to equal the service's own values for all 15) | The Hanafi count of 14 and its note; per-tradition explanations |
| The Arabic from the surah reader's cache | The service's own Arabic (a different edition) |

## Data

`app/data/sajda.generated.ts` (15 `[surah, ayah]` pairs; no text, no flags), generated by `scripts/gen-sajda.mjs` from `tests/fixtures/alquran-sajda.json` (a recorded real response of `/v1/sajda/quran-uthmani`). A test compares the two and checks that every pair is a real ayah, in order, and that juz and page match the service's. The page makes no request to the service's sajda endpoint.

The Arabic loads one ayah at a time (the same source and cache as the Library's snippets: the cached chapter, then the `ayah-text` store, then one small request), never a whole surah.

## Behaviour

- **Save:** the legacy bookmark `sajda:<surah>:<ayah>` (and its added-at time) with Undo. The Library lists it under "Sajda verses" and opens the reader at that ayah.
- **The text already carries the sign.** The reader's Arabic (`arabic1`) contains the prostration character inside exactly these 15 ayahs (checked on 2026-10-21), so this list, the surah reader and the Per-page reader show it as part of the text.
- **The sign on the Per-page reader (built, under review):** after each of these 15 ayahs the page reader shows a small mark (an SVG, `aria-label` "Prostration verse"). A test visits the page of each of the 15 and checks the sign is on exactly that ayah and nowhere else; pages without one show none. Because the text carries its own sign, this mark is a duplicate; removing the visible mark is recommended (phase doc).

## States

| State | What shows |
| --- | --- |
| Text loading | A skeleton line per row |
| Text not saved, offline | "Connect once to see the text." (the reference stays) |
| Text request failed | "Couldn’t load the text." and Try again |

## Data and storage

Reads: the bundled list, the reader's caches, `quran:bookmarks:v1`. Writes: `quran:bookmarks:v1` (+ added-at), the `ayah-text` cache. `quran-app-db` is never opened.
