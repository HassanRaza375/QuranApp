## Differences from the prototype (deliberate)

| Prototype | Revamp | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 26 events with descriptions and dates | The 29 events of the legacy data (and any reviewed-file entry), each placed on the day the calendar service reports | No invented events or descriptions; the prototype's list is a mock |
| First/Second Fatimiyya, Rajab begins, Birth of Imam Ali, Martyrdom of Imam Musa al-Kadhim, Birth of Fatima, Night of 19 Ramadan and others | Not shown | Not in the data; add them with a source in `reviewed-events.json` |
| "Sunni:" / "Ja'fari:" wording per event | Only the data's own note, or a reviewed file's wording with its source | No per-tradition wording without a source |
| Both, Sunni, Ja'fari toggle always visible | A "Show" menu; default Both when no fiqh is set, otherwise Following | Approved decision 4 |
| Dates as written ("Expected 16 or 17 May") | "Expected Sunday 16 May 2027 · 10 Dhū al-Ḥijjah 1448 · in 67 days" for every row | Approved decision 3 |
| Bell with no stated effect | "Saved to your list" with Undo and a visible "Your reminders" list; "No alert is sent" | Approved decision 6 |
| Footer: "...a reference, not a ruling. Follow your own marja'..." | "Dates and this list follow this app's data. They are not a ruling." | Ours, minimal, flagged |
| Month names plain | The calendar service's own names, with its diacritics | Not retyped |
